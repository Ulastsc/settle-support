Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your level progress, stars and sound setting are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped),
  • crash and performance diagnostics of the ad SDK.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. Change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you accept or refuse personalised ads.
  • You can play every part of the game whatever you choose.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Support

How to play

Swipe in any direction and every tile slides together until it hits a wall, the board's edge or another tile. Settle each tile on a ring of its own color, all at once. The par is the fewest moves possible; match it for three stars.

Stuck?

Tap Hint to see the best next move. Each level gives you one free hint, and you can watch an optional ad for more. Undo and Restart are always free.

Daily puzzle

The Daily tab has one puzzle per day, the same for everyone. Solve it and tap Share to send your result without giving away the answer.

Progress

Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
