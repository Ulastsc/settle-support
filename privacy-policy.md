# Settle / Kaydır — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

Settle (Kaydır in Turkish) is a puzzle game for iPhone, made by Ulaş Taşçıoğlu ("we").
This policy explains what data the game handles.

## What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your level progress,
stars and sound setting are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may
collect and process:

- your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt),
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- ad interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped),
- crash and performance diagnostics of the ad SDK.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Your choices**

- **Tracking:** iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose *Ask App Not to Track*,
  ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in
  *Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland):** a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform
  lets you accept or refuse personalised ads.
- You can still play every part of the game whatever you choose.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data
from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
