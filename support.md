# Settle / Kaydır — Support

**How to play:** swipe in any direction and every tile slides together until it hits a wall,
the board's edge or another tile. Settle each tile on a ring of its own color, all at once.
The par is the fewest moves possible; match it for three stars.

**Stuck?** Tap *Hint* to see the best next move. Each level gives you one free hint, and you
can watch an optional ad for more.

**Daily puzzle:** the *Daily* tab has one puzzle per day, the same for everyone. Solve it and tap
*Share* to send your result without giving away the answer.

**Progress:** stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**Ads and privacy:** see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Contact:** ulstsc00@gmail.com — bug reports, ideas and questions are welcome.
